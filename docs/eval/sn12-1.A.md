3. “And what, bhikkhus, is `paṭiccasamuppādo` (dependent `samudayo` (origination))? Conditioned by ignorance, bhikkhus, are `saṅkhārā` (mental constructions); conditioned by mental constructions is `viññāṇaṃ` (consciousness); conditioned by consciousness is `nāmarūpaṃ` (phenomenal objects); conditioned by phenomenal objects are the `saḷāyatanaṃ` (six sense bases); conditioned by the six sense bases is `phasso` (contact); conditioned by contact is `vedanā` (feeling); conditioned by feeling is `taṇhā` (craving); conditioned by craving is `upādānaṃ` (clinging); conditioned by clinging is `bhavo` (existence); conditioned by existence is `jāti` (birth); conditioned by birth, `jarāmaraṇaṃ` (old age and death), sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair come to be. Such is the origin of this entire `dukkhakkhandha` (mass of suffering). This, bhikkhus, is called dependent origination.”

4. “But with the complete fading away and `nirodho` (cessation) of `avijjā` (ignorance), mental fabrications end; with the cessation of mental fabrications, consciousness ends; with the cessation of consciousness, name-and-form ends; with the cessation of name-and-form, the six sense bases end; with the cessation of the six sense bases, contact ends; with the cessation of contact, feeling ends; with the cessation of feeling, craving ends; with the cessation of craving, clinging ends; with the cessation of clinging, existence ends; with the cessation of existence, birth ends; with the cessation of birth, `jarāmaraṇaṃ` (old age and death), sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair dissolve. Such is the cessation of this entire `dukkhakkhandha` (mass of suffering).” This is what the Bhagavā said. Delighted, those bhikkhus rejoiced in the words of the Bhagavā.
