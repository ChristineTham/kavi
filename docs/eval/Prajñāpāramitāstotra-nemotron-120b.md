# Prajñāpāramitāstotra (Perfection of Wisdom Hymn)

## Rāhulabhadra: Prajñāpāramitāstotra

`nirvikalpe` (unconceptualized one), homage to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (immeasurable one)  
who you are `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (one without fault in any limb) by the unblemished ones is seen.

Space like unstained proliferationless letterless  
who you object sees with disposition he sees Tathāgata.

Of you teacher of qualities of Buddha and of world-teacher  
not see between peaceful ones as moon and moonlight like.

Compassion-made fall to you object buddha-dharma-city-essence  
happily go greatness unmatched `bhaktivatsale` (devotion-loving one).

Once too in pure abode who you object ritual-wise sees  
by that too certainly attainment is obtained `amoghadarśane` (unfailing-seeing one).

All of even heroes self-for-others-purpose-natured  
nourisher and producer mother you are affectionate.

Since Buddhas world-teachers sons your compassionate ones  
by that you too `kalyāṇi` (auspicious one) all-beings-grandmother.

All-perfections by pure ones `anindite` (unblamed one)  
moon-mark like stars by followed always are.

Trainable people having approached there there Tathāgatas  
many-formed you indeed only one various-names-by worshipped are.

Radiance obtaining like shining-ray's certainly-drop-bubbles  
you object having obtained dissolution go faults and speeches of speakers.

You indeed fear-producing children-of terrible-seeing-one  
reassurance-producing also are of-knowers gentle-seeing-one.

Whose in you too attachment your-lord's not is known  
of him Amba how elsewhere passion-hatred will-be.

Not come from anywhere you not and nowhere go  
places in also all by knowers not found-are.

Who you thus not see fall to and becoming-from  
fall to and are-released that this great wonderful.

You indeed is-bound seeing not-seeing even is-bound  
you indeed is-released seeing not-seeing even is-released.

Oh wonderful-are deep-are `yaśasvini` (famous one)  
very-hard-to-understand-are illusion-like seen-are and not seen-are.

Buddhas by Pratyekabuddhas by and Śrāvakas by worshipped  
path you only release-of not other thus certainty.

Convention having-placed designation-purpose of embodied ones  
compassion-by Lokanāthas by called are and not called-are.

Able who you here to-praise signless stainless  
all-speech-subject-transcended who you somewhere non-attached.

Truly thus even convention speech-paths we such-like  
you object praise-in also being they-praise fully-contented.

Perfection-of-wisdom having-praised what by me collected good  
by that be soon world all perfection-of-wisness-going.
