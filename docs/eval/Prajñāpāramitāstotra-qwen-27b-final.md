# Prajñāpāramitāstotra (Hymn to the Perfection of Wisdom)

## Rāhulabhadra: Prajñāpāramitāstotra

`nirvikalpe` (indifferent), to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (incomparable)  
who, you, `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (having all faultless parts), with the faultless ones, you will observe.

Like space, without attachment, without proliferation, without letters  
who sees you by conception, that one sees the Tathāgata.

Of you, O teacher, and of the Buddha, and of the world-teacher  
they do not see an interval, existing, like the moon and its rays.

Having compassion as their nature, taking refuge in you, the foremost of the Buddha's Dharma  
they go to happiness, the incomparable greatness, `bhaktivatsale` (loving-kindness abode).

Even once, in a pure aspiration, who looks at you properly  
by that too, certainly, success is attained, `amoghadarśane` (infallible vision).

Of all even the heroes, whose selves are devoted to others' welfare  
you are the nourisher and mother, compassionate.

Since the Buddhas, world-teachers, are your sons, compassionate  
by that too, you, `kalyāṇi` (auspicious one), are the great-grandmother of all beings.

With all perfections, you, pure, `anindite` (blameless one)  
like a moonbeam, you are followed by stars always.

Having reached the people to be trained, there and there, by Tathāgatas  
you indeed, of many forms, alone, are praised by various names.

Like having reached light, the rays of the sun, and drops of water  
reaching you, faults go to dissolution, and disputes too, of disputants.

You indeed are the generator of fear for the foolish, of terrifying vision  
and you are the generator of reassurance for the wise, of gentle vision.

Of whom, even in you, there is no clinging to your lord  
of him, O mother, how will passion and hatred exist elsewhere.

You do not come from anywhere, and you do not go anywhere  
in places too, and in all, by the wise, you are not apprehended.

Who do not see you thus, and take refuge in existence  
and taking refuge, they are released; this is a great wonder.

You indeed are bound, seeing; not seeing too, one is bound  
you indeed are released, seeing; not seeing too, one is released.

Oh, you are astonishing, you are profound, `yaśasvini` (glorious one)  
you are hard to understand, like illusion you appear, and you do not appear.

By Buddhas, and by pratyekabuddhas, and by śrāvakas, it is prohibited  
you are the path; for release there is no other, this is certain.

Putting convention forward, for the meaning of designation of embodied beings  
by compassion, by lokanāthas, you are spoken of, and you are not spoken of.

Who is able to praise you here, without sign, without stain  
beyond all speech's domain, who, you, are unattached anywhere.

Even though it is true, by convention, by paths of speech, we, such ones  
you, not praised, even being present, praising, are well satisfied.

Having praised the perfection of wisdom, what merit I have accumulated  
by that too, may the entire world quickly become devoted to the perfection of wisdom.
