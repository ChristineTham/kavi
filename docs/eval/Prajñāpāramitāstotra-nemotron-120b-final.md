# Prajñāpāramitāstotra (Perfection of Wisdom Hymn)

## Rāhulabhadra: Prajñāpāramitāstotra

`nirvikalpe` (unconceptualized one), homage to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (immeasurable one)  
who you are `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (one whose limbs are faultless) by the faultless ones is seen.

Space like unstained proliferationless letterless  
who you sees with regard to that he sees Tathāgata.

Of you teacher of qualities of Buddha and of world-teacher  
not see between peaceful ones as moon and moonlight like.

Compassion-made fall you to the city of Buddha-dharma  
easily go greatness unmatched `bhaktivatsale` (devotion-loving one).

Once too in a pure abode who you according to rule sees  
by that too certainly success is obtained `amoghadarśane` (unfailing-seeing one).

Of all too of heroes of others-purpose-steadfast-selves  
nourisher and producer mother you are affectionate.

Since Buddhas world-teachers sons your compassionate ones  
by that you too `kalyāṇi` (auspicious one) all-beings-great-grandmother.

With all-perfections you immaculate `anindite` (unblamed one)  
moon-mark like with stars accompanied always are.

Trainable people having approached there there Tathāgatas  
many-formed you indeed alone by various names worshipped are.

Radiance obtaining like of the shining-rayed certainly drops fall  
you having obtained destruction go faults and speeches of speakers.

You indeed fear-producing of children terrible-seeing  
reassurance-producing also are of knowers gentle-seeing.

Of whom in you too attachment to your lord not is known  
of him amba how elsewhere passion-hatred will be.

Not come from anywhere you not and nowhere go  
in places too also by all knowers not found are.

Who you thus not see fall into existence and  
fall into and released that this great wonderful is.

You indeed bound seeing not-seeing too bound is  
you indeed released seeing not-seeing too released is.

Oh wondrous-are deep-are `yaśasvini` (famous one)  
very-hard-to-understand-are as-magic seen are not and seen are.

By Buddhas by pratyekabuddhas and by śrāvakas and worshipped  
path you alone of release no other is thus determination.

Convention having placed designation-purpose for embodied ones  
by compassion by lokanāthas you are called and not also called are.

Able who you here to praise signless stainless  
all-speech-subject-transcended who you where anywhere unrefuge.

Truly thus even by convention speech-paths we suchlike  
you in praise too being praised satisfied are.

Perfection of wisdom having praised what by me accumulated good  
by that let soon world all perfection-of-wonder-gone.
