# Perfection of Wisdom Hymn

## Rāhulabhadra: Perfection of Wisdom Hymn

`nirvikalpe` (non-dual one), homage to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (immeasurable one)  
who you, `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (one with all faultless limbs), are observed by the faultless.

Like space, unstained, without proliferation, without letters,  
he who sees you by essence, he sees the Tathāgata.

Of you, noble one, rich in qualities, and of the Buddha, the teacher of the world,  
they do not see a difference, like the moon and the moonlight.

Those whose nature is compassion, having approached you, the foremost of the Buddha-dharma,  
attain the immeasurable greatness easily, `bhaktivatsale` (affectionate toward devotion).

Even once, in a pure disposition, he who observes you according to the rule,  
by that even, success is certainly attained, `amoghadarśane` (one of unfailing vision).

Of all heroes whose self is devoted to the benefit of others,  
you are the nourisher, the generator, and the mother, affectionate one.

Since the Buddhas, the teachers of the world, are your children of compassion,  
therefore you even, `kalyāṇi` (auspicious one), are the grandmother of all beings.

By all perfections, by the pure ones, `anindite` (irreproachable one),  
like the crescent moon, you are followed by the stars always.

Having found the people to be led, there and there, by the Tathāgatas,  
you indeed, the one, are praised by the many in many forms.

Like the light of the sun, having been attained, which is brilliant,  
having attained you, faults and the arguments of the arguers go to destruction.

You indeed are the generator of consolation for children, she of frightening vision,  
and you are the generator of reassurance for the wise, she of gentle vision.

Of whom even in you, for that lord, no disturbance exists,  
for him, amba, how could passion and hatred be elsewhere?

You do not come from anywhere, and you do not go anywhere;  
even in all places, by the wise, you are not perceived.

Those who do not see you thus, and approach by essence,  
having approached, they are released; that this is a great wonder.

You indeed are bound, seeing; even not seeing, one is bound;  
you indeed are released, seeing; even not seeing, one is released.

Oh, you are astonishing, you are profound, `yaśasvini` (glorious one),  
you are difficult to understand, like an illusion you are seen and you are not seen.

Having been practiced by Buddhas, by pratyekabuddhas, and by śrāvakas,  
you are the one path of release; there is no other, this is the certainty.

Having placed convention foremost, for the sake of the designation of those with bodies,  
by the lokanāthas, you are called by compassion and you are not called.

Who is able here to praise you, the one without signs, the unstained,  
the one beyond the scope of all speech, who you are dependent on nowhere?

Truthfully, even so, by the paths of speech such as these, by convention, we,  
although you are unpraisable, having praised, are well satisfied.

Having praised the perfection of wisdom, whatever auspiciousness has been accumulated by me,  
by that, may the whole world quickly become devoted to the perfection of wisdom.
