# Perfection of Wisdom Hymn

## Rāhulabhadra: Perfection of Wisdom Hymn

`nirvikalpe` (undifferentiated) salutations to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (immeasurable),  
who, `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (all-faultless-limbed), are observed by the faultless ones.

Like space, unsullied, free from proliferation, and without a sign;  
who sees you, he sees a Tathāgata by way of being.

Of you, teacher, of the one possessing many virtues, of the Buddha and of the world-teacher;  
they do not see the interior, existing like the moon and the moon-light.

Compassionate ones, they take refuge in you, the ocean of the Buddha-dharma;  
`bhaktivatsale` (devotion-attached), they approach with ease the incomparable greatness.

Even in a single instance, in pure resolve, who observes you according to the rule;  
`amoghadarśane` (unfailing-visioned), by that even certain success is attained.

Of all the heroes, of those devoted to the sake of others;  
you are the nurturer, the creator, and the mother, affectionate.

Since the Buddhas, the world-teachers, are the sons of your compassion;  
`kalyāṇi` (auspicious), by that you too are the great-grandfather of all beings.

In all perfections, you, `anindite` (unblamable), through the pure ones;  
like the moon-rays, are you always followed by the stars.

Having brought the people to submission, there and there, by the Tathāgatas;  
indeed you are one, of many forms, `yaśasvini` (illustrious), praised by various ones.

Like the light, approaching the dewdrops of the shining ones;  
having attained you, the errors and the doctrines of the disputants approach destruction.

Indeed you are the mother of those who seek refuge, of the children of terrible sight;  
and you are the mother of those who seek refuge, of the wise, of gentle sight.

Of whom, even in you, attachment to your nātha is not found;  
amba, of him, where else shall passion and hatred exist?

You do not come from anywhere, and indeed you go nowhere;  
even in places, and by all the wise, you are not perceived.

Who do not see you thus, they indeed take refuge;  
and having taken refuge, they are released, this great and wonderful thing.

You indeed are bound, seeing, and even seeing, you are bound;  
you indeed are released, seeing, and even seeing, you are released.

Oh, `yaśasvini` (illustrious), you are astonishing, you are profound;  
you are difficult to be known, you are indeed seen, and not seen.

By the Buddhas, by the pratyekabuddhas, and by the śrāvakas, you are served;  
the path, you are the one, the certainty is that there is no other of the release.

Taking convention as the foremost, for the sake of the designation of the bodies;  
through compassion, you are spoken of by the lokanāthas, and not spoken of.

Who is able here to praise you, signless and unsullied;  
who, surpassing all speech, is indeed relied upon somewhere.

Even so, in truth, by the ways of speech such as these;  
while praising you, the satisfied ones, having been well-restrained.

Having praised the prajñāpāramitā, the good which was accumulated by me;  
by that, may the entire world indeed be quickly devoted to the perfection of wisdom.
