# Prajñāpāramitāstotra

## Rāhulabhadra: Prajñāpāramitāstotra

`nirvikalpe` (inconceivable), namas to you `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (infinite)  
who you `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (all-blameless-limbed) are seen by blameless.

Like space unattached, without proliferation, without letters  
who sees you with feeling, he sees Tathāgata.

Of you and of Buddha and of the world teacher  
the wise do not see a difference like of moon and moonlight.

Compassionate in nature, taking refuge in you, Buddha-dharma as leader  
happily they go to the incomparable greatness `bhaktivatsale` (devotion-affectionate).

Even in a pure desire once, who sees you rightly  
by him too certainly attainment is obtained `amoghadarśane` (unfailing-seeing).

Of all heroes too, whose self is engaged in others' welfare  
you are nourisher, producer and mother affectionate.

Since Buddhas, world teachers, your sons are compassionate  
therefore you too `kalyāṇi` (auspicious) are the grandmother of all beings.

By all perfections you are pure `anindite` (blameless)  
like moon disc you are always followed by stars.

Having obtained the people to be tamed, there there by Tathāgatas  
you alone in many forms are worshipped by different names.

Like drops of sweat of a shining sun  
having obtained you, faults and arguments of arguers go to dissolution.

You indeed are fear-producer for children, fearful in appearance  
and you are comfort-producer and are gentle in appearance for the wise.

Of whom in you too there is no embrace of your lord  
amba, how will passion and hatred be elsewhere for him.

You do not come from anywhere, and you also do not go anywhere  
and in all places too you are not perceived by the learned.

Who do not see you thus and take refuge with feeling  
and having taken refuge are released, this is great wonder.

Seeing you indeed he is bound, not seeing too he is bound  
seeing you indeed he is released, not seeing too he is released.

Oh you are wonderful, you are deep `yaśasvini` (glorious)  
you are hard to know, like illusion you appear and you do not appear.

By Buddhas, pratyekabuddhas and śrāvakas served  
you are the only path of release, there is no other, this is certainty.

Having placed convention before, for the sake of designation of embodied ones  
by compassion you are said by lokanāthas and you are not said.

Who can praise you here, without sign, without stain  
beyond all speech range, who you are nowhere dependent.

Truly even by convention by such speech of ours  
we praise you though unpraisable, being well content.

Having praised perfection of wisdom, what auspicious has been accumulated by me  
by that may the whole world quickly be devoted to the far shore of wisdom.
