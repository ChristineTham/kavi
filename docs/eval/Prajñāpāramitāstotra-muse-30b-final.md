# Prajñāpāramitāstotra (Perfection of Wisdom Hymn)

## Rāhulabhadra: Prajñāpāramitāstotra

`nirvikalpe` (unconceptualized), homage to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (boundless)  
who you, `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (all-faultless-limbed), are observed by faultless ones.

Like space unstained without-proliferation without-letters  
who sees you with feeling, he sees Tathāgata.

Of you and of Buddha and of world teacher the qualities abounding  
wise ones do not see a difference like of moon and moonlight.

Compassion-natured having taken refuge in you with Buddha-dharma in front  
easily come to incomparable greatness, `bhaktivatsale` (devotion-affectionate).

Even once in a pure aspiration who observes you with proper means  
by that too certainly attainment is obtained, `amoghadarśane` (unfruitless-seeing).

Of all heroes too whose self is engaged in others' welfare  
nourisher and begetter and mother you are affectionate.

Whom Buddhas world teachers your sons compassionate  
by that you too, `kalyāṇi` (auspicious), are the great-grandmother of all beings.

By all perfections of wisdom you are pure, `anindite` (blameless)  
like moon's disc you are always accompanied by stars.

Having obtained the people to be trained there there by Tathāgatas  
you alone in many forms by many names are worshipped.

Like having obtained the splendour of the sun's rays  
having obtained you, faults and arguments of arguers go to dissolution.

You indeed are the producer of fear for children fearful in appearance  
and you are the producer of reassurance for the learned gentle in appearance.

Of whom in you too there is no embrace of your lord  
O amba, how will passion and hatred exist elsewhere for him.

You do not come from anywhere and you also do not go anywhere  
in all places too and by the learned you are not perceived.

Who do not see you thus and take refuge in you with feeling  
and having taken refuge are released, this is a great wonder.

You indeed are bound by seeing, not seeing too is bound  
you indeed are released by seeing, not seeing too is released.

Oh how wonderful you are, deep you are, `yaśasvini` (glorious)  
you are hard to know like a magic and you appear and you do not appear.

By Buddhas by pratyekabuddhas and by śrāvakas served  
you alone are the path of release, there is no other, this is certain.

Having placed convention before for the purpose of designation of embodied ones  
by compassion you are called by lokanātha and you are not called.

Who can praise you here without sign without dust  
beyond all speech range who you are nowhere dependent.

Truly even so by convention by words of us such  
you being unpraiseworthy too we praise, being well content.

Having praised perfection of wisdom the auspiciousness accumulated by me  
by that may the whole world quickly be devoted to the far shore of perfection of wisdom.
