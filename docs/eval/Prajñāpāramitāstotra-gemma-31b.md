# Perfection of Wisdom Hymn

## Rāhulabhadra: Perfection of Wisdom Hymn

`nirvikalpe` (non-dual one), salutations to you, `prajñāpāramite` (perfection of wisdom), `amite` (immeasurable one)  
who you, `sarvānavadyāṅgi` (one with all faultless limbs), are beheld by the faultless.

Like space, unstained, without proliferation, without letters,  
he who beholds you by nature, he beholds the Tathāgata.

The qualities of you, noble one, rich in virtues, and those of the Buddha, the world-teacher,  
they do not see a difference, like the moon and the moonlight.

Those who are compassionate, having approached you, the foremost of the Buddha-dharmas,  
they come easily to the incomparable greatness, `bhaktivatsale` (one affectionate in devotion).

Even once, in a pure aspiration, he who beholds you according to the rule,  
by him even certainly success is attained, `amoghadarśane` (one of unfailing vision).

For all the heroes who are devoted to the benefit of others,  
you are the nourisher, the generator, and the mother, affectionate.

Since the Buddhas, the world-teachers, are your compassionate sons,  
therefore you even, `kalyāṇi` (auspicious one), are the grandmother of all beings.

By all the perfections, by the pure ones, `anindite` (irreproachable one),  
like the crescent moon, you are followed by the stars always.

Having found the people to be led, there and there, by the Tathāgatas,  
you indeed, the one, in many forms, are praised by the various ones.

Like the water-drops that must necessarily go to the blazing light of the sun having attained it,  
having attained you, the faults and the arguments of the arguers go to destruction.

You indeed are the generator of terror for children, of frightening vision,  
and you are the generator of reassurance for the wise, of gentle vision.

For him who has even no clinging to you, who has no nātha,  
for him, ambā, how could passion and hatred be elsewhere?

You do not come from anywhere, and you do not go anywhere;  
even in all places, by the wise, you are not perceived.

Those who do not behold you thus, and approach by nature,  
having approached, they are released; that this is a great wonder.

You indeed are bound, beholding; not beholding even, one is bound.  
You indeed are released, beholding; not beholding even, one is released.

Oh, you are astonishing, you are profound, `yaśasvini` (glorious one),  
you are difficult to understand, like magic you are seen and you are not seen.

Having been associated with by Buddhas, by pratyekabuddhas, and by śrāvakas,  
you are the one path of release; there is no other, this is the certainty.

Putting convention foremost, for the sake of designation for those in bodies,  
by the lokanāthas, out of compassion, you are spoken of and you are not spoken of.

Who is able here to praise you, the signless, the unstained,  
who is beyond the scope of all speech, who you are anywhere without dependence?

Truthfully, even so, by the paths of speech such as these, through convention, we,  
having praised you, though not praisable, are satisfied and well-content.

Having praised the perfection of wisdom, whatever auspiciousness has been accumulated by me,  
by that may the entire world quickly become devoted to the perfection of wisdom.
